16. Each of 10 students has a ticket to one of ten chairs in a row at a theater. How many ways 
are there to seat the students so that each student sits either in the chair specified on his/her 
ticket or in one right next to it? Each chair is to be occupied by exactly one student. 
 
A. 89   B. 144  C. 243  D. 512  E. 1024 
 
Answer: (a)  
 
Solution: Let F(n) be the number of ways to seat n students in a row of n chairs as described in 
the problem. The student who has the ticket to seat number n has two options. He/she can 
either sit in his/her own chair, in which case the remaining n -  1 students can arrange 
themselves in F(n - 1) ways. Alternatively, if this student takes the (n - 1)st seat, the student with

the ticket to that chair is forced to take the nth seat, and the other n -  2 students can be seated 
in F(n - 2) ways. Thus, F(n) = F(n -  1) + F(n  2), i.e. this is a sequence of Fibonacci numbers. It  
is easy to see that F(1) = 1 and F(2) = 2. Therefore, F(10) = 89, the 10thFibonacci number.
